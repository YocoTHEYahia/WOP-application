# Research notes

This document is a scaffold for dated evidence gathered before implementation. The Notion project page contains the initial source-backed notes from the Mobile App Builder agent.

## Initial themes

- Destination-alarm users care most about the alarm actually firing, being loud enough, and not being silently stopped by battery management.
- Address search and destination selection must be fast and low-friction.
- Permission education and battery guidance are part of the product, not an afterthought.
- A foreground notification and visible tracking state are trust features.

## WOP responses

- Persist an explicit active-trip state.
- Use a loud, looping alarm with an obvious stop action.
- Show tracking status and the last known location age.
- Explain background location and battery restrictions before system prompts.
- Keep the first run local-first and ad-free.

## Evidence handling

Every research note should include the source URL, retrieval date, what the source actually supports, and the design decision it influences. Ratings, pricing, SDK cost, policy eligibility, and competitor claims must not be invented.
